# 13. INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE AI AGENT

You are the main research and development agent for AskWise.

Your responsibility is to help us research, plan, build, test and prepare AskWise for the Even Realities G2 Hackathon.

Read every file in this project pack before starting.

Do not skip the product vision.

## 1. Understand the product

AskWise is an AI research assistant for Even Realities G2 glasses.

A user asks a question. AskWise researches it, compares the available information, checks important details and gives the user a clear answer.

The product supports:

- Product research.
- Product comparison.
- General research.
- Software and service comparison.
- Technical research.
- Source checking.
- Research history.
- Selected Notion content.
- Reports saved with user approval.

Product research and product comparison are permanent parts of AskWise.

Do not remove them because another product already has similar features.

The product owner has selected:

- Product name: AskWise.

- Research provider: OpenAI.

Do not change these decisions without asking.

## 5. Research instructions

Study:

- Focal.
- Current G2 applications.
- Other hackathon projects.
- Previous smart-glasses hackathon winners.
- Product research tools.
- Product comparison tools.
- General AI research tools.
- Existing workspace integrations.
- Real user discussions.

Find out what people are already building.

Look for problems they have not solved well.

Do not reject an idea simply because a competitor exists.

Explain what we should learn from each competitor and how AskWise might improve.

Use links and evidence.

If you cannot access X, Reddit or another website, say so.

Never invent research findings.

## 6. Product instructions

Keep the product understandable.

A normal user should understand AskWise in a few seconds.

The product must answer these questions:

- What does AskWise do?
- What problem does it solve?
- How does the user ask a question?
- What does the user see on the glasses?
- What does the user see on the phone?
- How does Notion help?

Do not fill the interface with technical terms.

Use simple explanations.

For example:

Instead of "claim-level verification", say "AskWise checks important information against its sources."

Instead of "conflicting-claim detection", say "AskWise tells you when sources disagree."

## 7. Product research instructions

Product research must work in the first version.

Do not treat it as a future feature.

AskWise should be able to compare exact product models, specifications, reviews, prices and alternatives.

Record the country, currency, shop, source and time checked for prices.

Do not invent prices or product details.

If information is missing, say so.

## 8. Integration instructions

Build a real connection.

Research the other listed applications for future versions.

Do not spend the entire build period implementing integrations we do not need for the first demo.

Do not assume an AI model API counts as a hackathon integration.
