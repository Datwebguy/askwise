# 03. FOCAL AND COMPETITOR RESEARCH

## 1. Why this research matters

Before building AskWise, study what other people have already built.

We need to understand:
- What works.
- What users like.
- What problems remain.
- What competitors do well.
- What AskWise should improve.

Do not reject an idea simply because another product has a similar feature.

Study it. Understand it. Find ways to make our product better.

Keep this research time-limited. We have a short build period.

## 2. Focal

Project:
https://devpost.com/software/focal-pi2qfe

Focal won the Even Realities Smart Glasses Building Challenge at the LA Hacks AI Hackathon 2026. This was a sponsor challenge, not an award from the current Even Realities hackathon.

Its Devpost page describes these features:

- Users ask questions through a wake phrase.
- Background agents research products.
- It compares prices and expert reviews.
- It looks for alternatives within the user's budget.
- A verifier checks prices.
- It presents three product choices.
- Users can add a choice to an Amazon cart with approval.
- It does not complete checkout.

Its reported technology includes Claude, FastAPI, Supabase, Fetch.ai agents and speech-to-text tools.

### What we need to learn from Focal

Investigate:

1. How does it identify exact product models?
2. How does it handle different product versions?
3. How does it check prices?
4. Does it handle prices in different countries?
5. How does it decide which reviews are reliable?
6. How does its G2 display work?
7. What does its phone experience show?
8. What are its limitations?
9. Which features should we avoid copying?
10. What improvements would make AskWise more useful?

Do not assume AskWise is better. Find evidence.

## 6. What AskWise should investigate improving

These are ideas to test, not claims of uniqueness:

- Better identification of exact product models.
- Better comparison of product versions.
- More reliable prices for the user's country.
- Clearer information about when prices were checked.
- Better separation of expert reviews and customer reviews.
- Checking whether sources support important claims.
- Showing when sources disagree.
- Letting users choose what matters most.
- Remembering previous research.
- Using selected personal notes as research context.
- Showing useful answers on G2 without overcrowding the display.

## 7. User demand

Look for real discussions on:
- Reddit.
- X.
- Product review websites.
- Smart-glasses communities.
- Developer communities.
- Product research forums.

Find out whether people complain about:
- Confusing product versions.
- Incorrect prices.
- Conflicting reviews.
- Too many search results.
- AI answers without reliable sources.
- Difficulty researching while working.

Include links to the discussions.

If a platform blocks access, report it. Do not invent user opinions.

## 8. Required report

Create COMPETITOR_RESEARCH_REPORT.md.

For every competitor, record:

- Name and link.
- What it does.
- Who uses it.
- Main features.
- How it researches information.
- How it compares products.
- How it checks prices.
- How it handles sources.
- Whether it works with smart glasses.
- Its integrations.
- Its strengths.
- Its verified weaknesses.
- What AskWise should learn or improve.

Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
