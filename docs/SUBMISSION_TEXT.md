## Project name
AskWise

## One-line description
Research anything, compare your options and know what to choose: a checked answer on your G2 glasses, with every source on your phone.

## Track
AI Assistants and Work

## Short description

Choosing a laptop, a phone or a software tool means hours of tabs: specs that don't match, prices that change from shop to shop, and reviews that disagree. AskWise does that research for you.

Ask on your phone and name your options and what matters to you. AskWise searches the web with OpenAI, reads official pages, reviews and shops, then checks every important fact against the page it came from. Facts without a source are never shown as confirmed, and prices are kept only when a real shop page shows them, with the currency, country, shop and time checked.

Your G2 glasses show what you need in the moment: the best pick, why, the main drawback, how strong the evidence is and when prices were checked. You follow the research live on the glasses and tap through the answer. Your phone holds the full report: a comparison table, specs, prices, reviews, checked facts, where sources disagree, and every source.

Notion is built in. AskWise can read a Notion page you choose, such as your budget and priorities, and it saves the finished report to Notion only after you review the exact content and approve it. Saving twice never creates a copy.

## Why G2
The answer to "which one should I choose?" is short. On G2 it sits in your line of sight in a shop, a meeting or on the move, while the phone keeps the detail for when you want it.

## Main integration
Notion: read a selected page, and save approved reports, with duplicate protection and revocable access.
