# 01. PRODUCT VISION

## 1. What is AskWise?

AskWise is an AI assistant for Even Realities G2 glasses.

You ask AskWise a question. It researches the topic, compares available information and gives you a clear answer.

It is designed for people who want to understand something or make a decision without spending hours searching different websites.

**Simple explanation: AskWise does the research. You get the answer and the sources.**

## 2. The problem we are solving

People often struggle when researching something.

For example:

- You want to buy a laptop but don't know which model to choose.
- You want to compare two phones.
- You need to find the best software for a project.
- You want to understand a technical topic.
- You find different prices on different websites.
- You read reviews that disagree.
- You see claims online but don't know whether they are true.

Most people open many tabs, read several articles and try to put everything together themselves.

AskWise should reduce this work.

## 3. What AskWise should do

### A. Product research and comparison

This is one of AskWise's main features.

Example:

"Compare three laptops for programming, school work and battery life. My budget is $900."

AskWise should:

- Find the exact laptop models.
- Check their specifications.
- Compare their features.
- Find independent reviews.
- Look for available prices.
- Identify the country and currency for each price.
- Explain the advantages and disadvantages.
- Find other options when useful.
- Recommend a product based on the user's needs.

If AskWise cannot confirm a detail, it must say so.

### B. General research

Example:

"Explain the current state of passkeys."

AskWise searches for useful information, checks important details and presents a simple explanation with sources.

### C. Software and website comparison

Example:

"Compare three project management tools for a small team."

AskWise compares:

- Features.
- Prices and plans.
- Supported devices.
- Integrations.
- Limitations.
- Ease of use.
- User feedback.

### D. Technical research

Example:

"Compare different ways to build a backend for this application."

AskWise researches technical options, documentation, costs, limitations and maintenance needs.

It must never invent technical benchmarks.

### E. Research with Notion

AskWise should read pages the user selects from Notion.

It can use those pages to understand the user's project, notes or requirements.

It must not read the entire account without permission.

### F. Research history

Users should be able to:

- Open previous reports.
- Ask more questions about previous research.
- Update an old comparison.
- Check whether prices or specifications have changed.
- Save useful reports.

### G. Saving reports

AskWise prepares a report and shows the user what it intends to save.

The user checks the content and approves it.

Only then does AskWise save it to Notion.

## 4. Why use smart glasses?

The glasses give users quick access to research while they are doing something else.

Examples:

- Comparing products while shopping.
- Checking information during work.
- Getting a quick answer without reaching for a phone.
- Comparing options during a discussion.

The glasses should show only the most useful information.

The phone should show the full report.

## 5. What the user sees

### On the glasses

- Main answer.
- Best option, when the evidence supports one.
- Main reason.
- One important disadvantage.
- Whether the information is well supported.
- An option to open the full report on the phone.

### On the phone

- Full explanation.
- Comparison table.
- Product specifications.
- Prices and when they were checked.
- Sources and links.
- Conflicting information.
- Missing information.
- Previous research.
- Notion connection and saving approval.

## 6. Our product rules

1. Product research stays a core feature.
2. Important information needs sources.
3. Exact product models and versions matter.
4. Prices need a country, currency and date checked.
5. AskWise must explain when information is uncertain.
6. Users decide what matters most in a comparison.
7. Users approve anything AskWise saves or sends.
8. The glasses show short answers. The phone shows details.

## 7. What AskWise should not become

- A generic chatbot with no proper research.
- An online shopping or checkout agent.
- An assistant that performs actions without permission.
- Another email application.
- A social media dashboard.
- An application that reads every connected account without permission.

## 8. Long-term vision

AskWise should support different kinds of research and connect to more useful services over time.

The first version should prove the main experience works. A smaller first release does not mean we are abandoning the wider product vision.
